# SOP: Building Local Visibility & Backlinks
### Lifestyle Photography by Tiffany — a simple, no-jargon playbook

Hi Tiffany! This is your step-by-step guide to getting your website and business found by more local families. You don't need to understand SEO — just follow the steps. Everything you need to copy-paste is in the other files in this folder.

**Time commitment:** ~2–3 hours in week one, then ~20–30 minutes a week. That's it.

---

## The big picture (read this once)

Getting found on Google comes down to three things for a photographer like you:

1. **Your Google Business Profile** (that map listing with your reviews) — ✅ you already have this.
2. **Reviews** — the more recent Google reviews you have, the higher you show up. You already have a solid base (8+), which is great — the goal now is simply to **keep a steady trickle coming** so it never goes stale. **This is the most important ongoing thing you can do.**
3. **Being listed & mentioned around the web** — on directories and local sites. This tells Google you're a real, established local business. That's what this folder helps you do.

You do **not** need to pay anyone for "backlink services." Those are scams that can actually hurt you. Everything here is free (or nearly free) and legit.

## ✅ Week 1 — The foundation (~2 hours, do it in a few sittings)

### Step 1 — Set up your review link (15 min) — DO THIS FIRST
This is the highest-impact thing you'll do all month.
1. Open your Google Business Profile.
2. Find **"Ask for reviews"** or **"Get more reviews"** → copy the short link it gives you.
3. Open `03-OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md`, paste it into **Template E**, and save.
4. From now on, **send Template E to every client 1–2 days after you deliver their gallery.** Put it in your gallery-delivery routine so you never forget.

### Step 2 — Double-check your Google Business Profile (15 min)
Make sure it shows:
- [ ] Phone: **(910) 779-8088**
- [ ] Website: **https://photobytiff.com**
- [ ] Categories: Photographer (+ Family/Newborn/Maternity if you can add them)
- [ ] At least 10 photos uploaded
- [ ] Your services and prices listed
- [ ] Hours set (even if "by appointment")

### Step 3 — Claim the core directories (60–90 min)
Open `02-DIRECTORY-CHECKLIST.md` and do all of **Tier 1**, copying your info from the Citation Kit:
- [ ] Bing Places
- [ ] Apple Business Connect
- [ ] Yelp
- [ ] Nextdoor Business
- [ ] (Confirm Facebook & Instagram have your website + correct info)

💡 Tip: Bing and Apple can often **import your info straight from Google** — look for that option to save time.

## ✅ Weeks 2–4 — Expand (~30 min/week)

Keep working down `02-DIRECTORY-CHECKLIST.md`:
- **Tier 2** (photographer sites): Thumbtack, Bark, The Bash, and WeddingWire/The Knot if you do any couples work. These also send you real leads.
- **Tier 3** (general directories): Yellow Pages, Manta, BBB, etc. — a few each week.

Do 3–5 per sitting. Don't burn out trying to do them all at once.

## ✅ Ongoing — The habits that compound (a little each week)

### Every single session:
- [ ] Send the **review request** (Template E) after delivering the gallery. **This is non-negotiable — it's your #1 growth lever.**

### 1–2 times a week (15 min):
- [ ] Reach out to ONE local partner using `03-OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md`:
  - A **wedding/event venue** → Template A (ask to be on their preferred-vendor list)
  - A **cake maker / boutique / planner / doula** you'd recommend → Template B (mutual referral)
  - A **local family blog or Macaroni KID** → Template C (offer a guest article)
- [ ] Be active in **Fort Bragg spouse / Fayetteville mom Facebook groups** — be helpful, and occasionally share your work (Template D). Real relationships here turn into both bookings AND links.

### Monthly:
- [ ] Post to your Google Business Profile (a recent session, a mini-session announcement) — keeps the profile active, which Google likes.
- [ ] Check that new reviews are coming in. Reply to every review (thank them) — it helps ranking and looks great to future clients.

## ❌ Do NOT do these (they waste money or hurt you)
- Don't buy "500 backlinks" or cheap link/SEO gigs (Fiverr, spam emails offering to "rank you #1"). They're scams and can get you penalized.
- Don't use auto-submit tools that blast your info everywhere with tiny differences.
- Don't stress about doing everything at once. Slow and consistent beats a frantic weekend.
