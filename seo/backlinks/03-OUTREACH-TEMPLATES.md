# Outreach Email Templates

Copy, fill in the `[brackets]`, and send. Keep them short and warm — you're a local small business reaching out to another, not a marketer. Personalize the first line every time (mention something specific about them); generic copy-paste gets ignored.

**Before sending any of these:** send from tiffany@photobytiff.com, keep your email signature consistent (name, business, phone, website), and always link to https://photobytiff.com.

---

## Template E — Thank-you + review request (send after EVERY session)

*This isn't a backlink, but it's the single highest-impact thing on this whole list — Google reviews drive your map-pack ranking. Send it 1–2 days after delivering the gallery.*

> Hi [Client name]!
>
> It was so wonderful capturing [your family / baby [name] / your maternity session]! I hope you love your gallery as much as I loved photographing you.
>
> If you have 30 seconds, it would mean the world to me if you'd leave a quick Google review — it genuinely helps other local families find me:
> 👉 [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
>
> Thank you again for trusting me with these memories!
> Tiffany

**How to get your Google review link (do once):**
1. Go to your Google Business Profile → click **"Ask for reviews"** / **"Get more reviews."**
2. Google gives you a short link (like `g.page/r/...`). Copy it.
3. Paste it into Template E above and save. Optionally shorten it at tinyurl.com and add it to your gallery-delivery email so it goes out automatically.
