## How to gather the data (free)

1. Open an **incognito** browser window. Optionally set location to Fayetteville, NC (Chrome DevTools → Sensors → Location, or just search "...fayetteville nc").
2. Search each money keyword from SEO-STRATEGY §2.
3. Record who appears in the **Map Pack** (top 3 local results) and the **organic** results.
4. For each recurring competitor, open their site and note the columns below.

## Market hypotheses (validate, then keep/discard)

- **Most local competitors hide pricing.** You publish it → trust + conversion advantage. Confirmed on your own site; verify competitors.
- **Few will have a dedicated Fort Liberty / military-family page.** This is your wedge — PCS, homecoming, deployment, promotion-ceremony family sessions.
- **Directory/aggregator pages (Thumbtack, The Bash, Bark, WeddingWire) likely rank for several money terms.** You can't beat them on their own domains, so instead: get listed on them (referral + backlink), and out-rank them for *specific* service+town long-tails where they're weak.
- **Single-page competitors that lump all services together** are beatable on specific service terms with dedicated pages — same gap your current `/services` page has.
