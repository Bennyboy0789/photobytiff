## Repurposing workflow (per session)
1. Pick 5–8 favorite frames → blog post "recap" (with client permission).
2. 3 GBP posts spread over 2 weeks from the same set.
3. Instagram/Facebook carousel → link in bio to the matching service page.
4. Ask the client for a Google review (one-tap link) → feeds map-pack ranking.
